Formula SAE Chassis & Steering Work — HUAS

I was tasked with selecting an impact attenuator for the car’s front-impact structure and am currently working through the design review and approval process. My current work focuses on integrating the selected attenuator with the anti-intrusion plate and chassis, including how it will be mounted securely while meeting Formula SAE requirements.

Current Work

After evaluating impact attenuator options, I selected a commercially available foam attenuator that fits the car’s packaging and cost constraints. I am now refining the mounting design and working toward approval of the final configuration before fabrication and installation.

The current work includes positioning the attenuator at the front of the chassis, integrating it with the anti-intrusion plate, determining a secure mounting method, checking packaging and fit with the existing chassis, and preparing the design for review and approval.

Design Development

Current Status

The selected foam impact attenuator is now incorporated into the chassis CAD with the anti-intrusion plate. I am refining how the assembly mounts to the chassis and working toward design approval before fabrication and installation.
