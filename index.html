About

Designing for the real world.

I’m a first-generation Mechanical Engineering student at Harvard with Mexican and Guatemalan roots. I’ve always been drawn to hands-on work, so engineering clicked for me because it gives me a way to turn ideas into things I can actually build, test, and improve. I’m especially interested in automotive engineering, mechanical design, and product development, and I enjoy the entire process from CAD and prototyping to machining and seeing a finished part come together.

Outside of engineering, soccer is a huge part of my life. I play for Harvard Men’s Club Soccer and I’m a lifelong Chivas fan. I also spend time helping with my family’s catering business, which has been part of my life for years and has taught me a lot about working with people, handling responsibility, and staying composed when things get busy. My family and cultural background are a big part of who I am, and I try to bring that same sense of community and work ethic into what I do at Harvard.
